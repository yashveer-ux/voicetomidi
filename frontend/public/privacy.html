HUM to MIDI

Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: 1 MAY 2026

Short version: We do not store your audio recordings. We do not track you with cookies. We do not sell your data. We collect only anonymous usage statistics.

Who we are

HUM to MIDI (humtomidi.com) is a browser-based music creation tool. You can contact us at ysookun321@gmail.com.

Audio recordings

When you record your voice or import an audio file, it is sent to our server for pitch and rhythm analysis. We want to be clear about how this is handled:

  • Audio is processed in memory and immediately discarded after analysis — we do not write it to disk or any database.
  • We do not store, share, or sell your recordings.
  • Audio is transmitted over an encrypted HTTPS connection.

Analytics

We use Vercel Analytics to understand how many people visit the site and which pages are popular. It is:

  • Cookie-free — no tracking cookies are set on your device.
  • Anonymous — no personal identifiers are collected.
  • Aggregated — data is reported as totals, not individual sessions.

Cookies

We do not use cookies. None.

Third-party services

The app relies on a small number of external services:

  • Vercel — hosts the frontend. Vercel Privacy Policy
  • Railway — hosts the backend API. Railway Privacy Policy
  • Buy Me a Coffee — optional donation link. If you click it you will be taken to their site, governed by their privacy policy.

We do not share any data with these services beyond what is technically necessary to run the app.

Data we do not collect

  • Names, email addresses, or account information (there are no accounts)
  • Payment information (we do not process payments)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Your audio recordings (see above)

Your rights (GDPR)

If you are based in the European Economic Area, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation. Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to provide, correct, or delete. If you have questions, contact us at ysookun321@gmail.com.

Children

HUM to MIDI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance.

Contact
